Umbrella pattern

The Company Brain

Most teams don't start with "we need a context graph" — they start with "non-engineers should be able to build the workflows and small apps our business actually needs." The Company Brain is what makes that possible. Every function-level pattern below (Cloud FinOps, Sales Ops, Marketing Ops, Custom Agents) lives inside it.

Who this is for

The teams that benefit

Operators who know the business but can't code. Function leaders tired of waiting for a backlog. AI engineering teams who want one shared layer instead of rebuilding context infra for every team's request. The Brain serves all three.

Layer pieces

What the Brain gives them

Living Context Brain — resolved entities, curated knowledge, learned skills, and the trace of every run. Agent-Native Storage for the work agents do. Scanned Sandboxes for the small apps non-engineers build on top. Permissions so the same scope rules govern every agent and every app. Traces so the AI team sees what's running.

Outcome shape

What teams typically see

The backlog of "small internal tool" requests stops piling up. Non-engineers ship the 20% custom to your business that no SaaS will ever ship. Tribal knowledge stops dying in chat history — it gets captured and served back. Every new agent built on the Brain inherits everything earlier agents learned.

Sales Ops

Operating Layer for Sales Ops Agents

A sales-ops team running agents on top of the layer. The agents handle the busywork of pipeline hygiene, deal context, and forecast prep — so reps focus on selling and leadership sees signal instead of noise.

Example agents

What the team runs

Pipeline Health Monitor — flags stage transitions gone quiet. Stale Deal Flagger — surfaces opportunities without recent touch. Forecast Drafter — confidence-weighted weekly forecast. Deal Context Compiler — all the Slack + doc context on a deal, before a call.

Layer pieces

What the layer gives them

Context graph across CRM + Slack + docs + email — one resolved view of every deal, customer, contact. Skills for CRM updates, Slack nudges, doc generation — governed and observable. Trace loop learning which deal shapes convert and which stages are traps.

Outcome shape

What teams typically see

Forecast accuracy improves week over week as the layer learns the team's deal patterns. New reps inherit what the whole team has learned — onboarding compresses. Time spent on pipeline hygiene drops, time on actual deals goes up.

Marketing Ops

Operating Layer for Marketing Ops Agents

A growth-ops team running agents that span audience, creative, and spend across paid platforms. The non-engineering side of the team operates the agents; engineering ships the skills.

Example agents

What the team runs

Campaign Builder — drafts campaigns across Meta + Google from a brief. Audience Segmenter — builds and refreshes audiences off CDP + Shopify behavior. Creative Reviewer — checks creative against brand rules + past winners. Spend Optimizer — reallocates within bounded daily limits.

Layer pieces

What the layer gives them

Context graph across Meta, Google, Shopify, your CDP, past creative library. Skills for ad-platform actions — governed, bounded, audited. Sandboxes for spend changes — start in shadow, graduate to autonomous within daily caps. Trace loop on what creative + audience combinations actually convert.

Outcome shape

What teams typically see

Time-to-launch on new campaigns drops sharply. Non-engineering marketers self-serve agent workflows that previously needed engineering. Spend allocation tracks performance faster than manual review cycles. Creative learnings compound — losing variants stop being repeated.

Build your own

Operating Layer for Custom AI Agents

Building an AI product? Internal copilot? Agent for an unusual workflow your business runs on? Skip rebuilding the infra. Use the layer the same way the function teams above do.

Who this is for

The team

AI engineering teams shipping custom agents — either for an internal use-case your business actually depends on, or for an AI product your customers use. Bring your own harness (Claude Code, Cursor, in-house). Synos is the layer underneath.

Layer pieces

What the layer gives you

Streaming context graph — your SoRs, resolved and live. Skill registry + marketplace — every skill your team writes, governed and shareable. Sandboxes — safe execution surface. Trace loop — production runs feed back into your context. Self-hosted. Model-agnostic. Yours.

Outcome shape

What teams typically see

Skip rebuilding context, retrieval, governance, and trace infrastructure for every new agent. Ship your second agent in days, not weeks. The brain compounds across every agent you build on it — your AI products get sharper with every run.
