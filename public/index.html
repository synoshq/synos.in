Questions.

Do we need an ML or research team?

No. SynOS platform agents build the training environments, curate and clean the data and generate the training sets, which is the work a research team would otherwise do. A small team of domain experts and engineers runs it with support from SynOS.

Where do our data and the model live?

In your own cloud account. SynOS installs there and reads your systems in place. Traces, corrections, training data and model weights all stay inside your boundary.

What happens to the model and the data if we stop?

They stay in your account. The weights, evals, training data and traces are yours whether or not you carry on with SynOS.

How much expert time does it take?

Experts review and correct agent runs in the tools they already use, so the work looks like their normal review of output. How much time it takes depends on the use case and on how often the agent is wrong.

What does a first use case look like?

One workflow your experts already do, with a clear idea of what a correct result is. SynOS connects the systems it touches, agents run on live data with their writes recorded, and your experts' corrections start the eval set and the training data.

How is this different from a hosted fine-tuning API?

A fine-tuning API starts from a dataset you have already built and trains on the provider's infrastructure. SynOS builds the environment and the dataset from your systems and your experts' corrections, trains inside your cloud and gates each release on your evals.
