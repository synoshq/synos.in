What's inside the operating layer

A Living Context Brain. The agent-native pieces around it.

Sits between your systems of record, your agent harness, and your team. The brain is the anchor. The pieces around it are what make agents safe to run in production.

Anchor

Living Context Brain

Streaming ingestion from your systems of record. Smart extraction. Entity resolution. A context graph that updates itself as your business changes today. Remembers skills, entities, decisions, traces.

Self-Learning Loop: every agent trace + every human correction feeds back. The brain compounds — model-agnostic, harness-agnostic, yours forever.

Storage

Agent-Native Storage

A System of Record built for agents

Today's smart Harnesses need a shared place to track the work they do. The writing patterns, controls, and checks they need aren't what typical SQL or NoSQL stores were built for. Synos ships an SoR designed for how agents actually write, revise, and reference each other.

Sandboxes

Secure Scanned Sandboxes

Safe execution surface for agent actions

Sandboxed runs for the agents and apps your team builds. Scanned for what they touch. Graduated trust from shadow → approved → autonomous within bounded scopes.

Permissions

Tool Access & Permissions

Agent RBAC

Per-skill, per-agent, per-team scopes. Revocable. Auditable. Engineering provisions templates, not requests. Non-engineers self-serve within scope.

Analytics

Skill & Tool Traces

Analytics for your AI team

Every skill run, every tool call — captured. Cost, latency, success rate, hallucination signal. A dashboard your AI team can actually read.
