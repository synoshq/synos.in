Outcomes

What a team gets out of it.

Accuracy where prompting stopped working

The failures that survive every prompt rewrite are the ones where the agent never had the information. It is in your systems, and in the judgment of the people who do the work.

Agents you can let take actions

An agent rehearses against your systems as they are. The write it would have made is intercepted and recorded rather than committed, then scored against what should have happened.

Cost per run, itemised

Every run is priced per model and per provider, so you can see where the money goes before you change anything about how it is spent.

A model of your own, eventually

Corrections accumulate inside your estate in the shape a training set is built from, and no other company trains on them. The hard part of a model of your own was never the training run.

The platform

Four pieces, and they install together.

Each one is useful on the day it is switched on. The order below is the order they tend to be turned on in.

01 · Context

One resolved view of your systems

Connects warehouses, databases, applications and documents, profiles what is in them, and resolves the same customer across all of them. An agent reads that view instead of a prompt describing your schema.

02 · Access

One decision point for every tool call

Every call an agent makes is scoped to who is asking and recorded when it is made. It works with the agent framework your team already uses, and with the models you already pay for.

03 · Evaluation

Test cases your team writes

Replayed against your own systems rather than against a fixture, scored, and gated so a release that scores worse than the last one does not ship.

04 · Training data

Runs and corrections, curated

Traces, tool calls and the corrections your experts make in the tools they already use become a curated dataset, exported in the format your trainer reads. We build the environment and the dataset. Your trainer does the training.
