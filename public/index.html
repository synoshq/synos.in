Why this is hard

An agent needs somewhere it can act and be scored. Your business is not that place yet.

There are three ways the market builds one, and every one of them is a reconstruction. The reconstruction is accurate about the things that were written down, which are not the things your agent gets wrong.

What is actually yours

The inputs that decide the outcome are not for sale.

Compute and capable open models sit on a shelf now. The company competing with you rents the same accelerators and downloads the same weights, on the same afternoon, at the same price. What neither of you can buy is the other one’s systems, or the judgment of people who have run that work for a decade.

What you get out of it

Three things, and they arrive in that order.

Accuracy where prompting stopped working

The failures that survive every prompt rewrite are the ones where the agent never had the information. Your systems and your experts’ corrections are where that information is.

Agents you can let act

An agent rehearses against your systems as they are, its write intercepted and recorded rather than committed, and the action it would have taken is scored against what should have happened.

A corpus nobody else can assemble

Every correction and every scored run accumulates inside your estate, in the shape a training set is built from. The hard part of a model of your own was never the training run.
