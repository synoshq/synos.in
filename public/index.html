The Problem

Intelligent agents are here. Tools are here.
Your institutional brain isn't.

Agents can already take actions — draft, update, edit, send — across your systems. They just don't know your business well enough to do it reliably.

Today's AI tools can't be productionized for teams

+

Brilliant for individual productivity. Fragile for teams. No organizational memory. No RBAC. No safe, controlled tools for the systems that actually run your company. 89% of agent projects are stuck in pilots for exactly this reason.

Your institutional knowledge is scattered across apps, docs, and humans

+

Salesforce, BigQuery, Jira, Slack, Notion, critical spreadsheets, and the heads of a few key people. Custom ways of working, institutional rules, tribal knowledge — all fragmented. Nobody has the full picture, and no agent can draw from it.

Agents don't understand how your company actually operates

+

They can access your data, but they can't read your business. Your rules, your exceptions, your conventions, your judgment calls — none of it is in any system they can query. Without that context, agents guess. Confidently, and often wrongly.

no matter how smart the models get.

Build it once. Compound it forever. Models commoditize. Agent frameworks come and go. The institutional brain stays, accumulates, and compounds — model-agnostic, framework-agnostic, yours.

How SynOS Works

One institutional brain. Three things it does.

Bring your agent layer on top — or use the SynOS harness. These aren't read-only agents. They take action across your systems. Every agent draws from the same institutional brain.

Connect your systems. Resolve your entities.

Profiling agents automatically analyze your connected data sources. They extract schema, statistics, and business semantics — then discover cross-system relationships that no single system can see. "Customer ID" in Salesforce = "Client #" in the spreadsheet = @acmecorp in Slack. Automatically resolved. Runs continuously as your data evolves.

Every run makes every agent smarter.

Every time an agent executes, the system captures patterns, exceptions, and edge cases. Those learnings are written back into the brain layer and become available to every other agent. Fix a mistake once — every agent inherits it. Your 10th agent starts smarter than your 1st.

→ Agent executes a workflow
→ System captures patterns + exceptions
→ Quality gates filter noise
→ Learnings enter the brain layer
→ All agents inherit immediately

Capture what only your team knows.

The rules, exceptions, and judgment calls that never get documented. Your team corrects mistakes in plain language — one sentence at a time. Corrections pass through a quality gate and become permanent institutional memory. Tribal knowledge becomes organizational knowledge.
