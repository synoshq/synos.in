Trace collection + learning extraction → next agent does it better. Human corrections become organizational knowledge.

Living Company Brain

The cross-function brain your company compounds.

Each function — finance, sales, marketing, ops, engineering — builds its own agents on the layer. But the brain underneath is shared. The customer that Sales just signed is the same customer Finance is invoicing and Marketing is segmenting. Resolved once. Known everywhere.

That cross-cutting view is the Living Company Brain. It's not a separate product. It's what happens when every team builds their function-brain on the same operating layer — and the layer learns from all of them.

Start with one function. The brain compounds across the rest as you go.
