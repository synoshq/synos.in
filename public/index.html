Why this is the work now

Frontier models know what the world knows. They will never know how you operate.

Which fields mean what, which exceptions matter, who decides, what your people learned the hard way. None of that is on the internet and none of it is going to be. As models get better at everything general, the value left inside a company concentrates in the part only that company has.

That points somewhere specific. The firms that matter in ten years are the ones whose AI learned their business and whose learning stayed theirs. Doing it properly is a serious infrastructure investment, and almost no company should be attempting it in-house.

The handoff that usually kills these projects

The rules that make an agent correct live with people who do not write code.

Today those rules only reach production through an engineer, and that queue is where AI programmes die. SynOS splits the work at the place it should have been split all along.

engineering owns · set once

The rails

  • Connectors and data scopes: what an agent may read
  • Governed tools and write actions: what it may do, and what needs a human
  • Identity, permissions, audit, sandboxes, cost attribution
  • The check that has to pass before anything goes live

Built once and reviewed, rather than re-implemented for every request that arrives.

your experts own · every day

The knowledge

  • Skills in plain English: how we actually do this
  • The rules, exceptions and judgement calls, captured where they surface
  • Corrections on agent output, each one reaching every agent afterwards
  • Test cases: here are ten real ones, get them right

No code, no ticket, no waiting. Inside the rails there is nothing they can break.

What accumulates

The corrections your people make are the part no model can be trained on.

Not because they are secret, but because they only exist inside your work. A correction is reviewed once and then it is permanent: a rule every agent inherits, context in the brain, a case in your evals, and an example a model of your own can learn from.
