About

Building the institutional brain
for AI agents.

SynOS is founded by Anoop Jawahar.

I've spent my career at the intersection of data infrastructure and enterprise systems. At Sundial, I built semantic layers and big-data ETL pipelines — where I learned firsthand that accurate long-horizon agents depend on structured, multi-dimensional context, not bigger models. At Nutanix, I was a Director of Engineering leading distributed systems and storage infrastructure at enterprise scale. I published at NSDI and hold multiple patents in distributed key-value stores and adaptive cache management.

The institutional brain for AI agents is the same class of problem I've been solving in different forms for a decade: making the right data available to the right system at the right time. SynOS is that layer for the AI-native era.

Why this layer will always exist

Models will keep changing. Agent frameworks will come and go. But every company will always need a structured, living layer of its own institutional knowledge — the rules, exceptions, relationships, and judgment calls that define how it actually operates. That layer is the moat. It compounds over time, survives model churn, and stays yours no matter which harness you use.
