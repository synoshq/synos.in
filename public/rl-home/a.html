What stands in the way

Six things stand between your data and a model of your own.

Each one is work a research team would normally do before the first training run.

The training environment

Agents need somewhere to act on real systems without consequences. Copies and sandboxes drift away from the business they were copied from.

The data

Historical records are scattered across systems, inconsistent and unlabelled. What your experts know has never been written down as a dataset.

The reward

Someone has to decide what a correct result means for each task. That definition has to stay current as the business changes.

The team

Research engineers who build environments and run post-training are scarce, and most companies have none.

The loop

Corrections made in production rarely flow back into the model. The same mistake gets fixed by hand, again, next week.

Sovereignty

Training data cannot leave your boundary. Sending it to an outside trainer is often not an option.

The platform

Three components, installed in your cloud.

Platform agents do the environment building and the data cleaning. Your experts do what only they can: say what correct looks like.

Questions

What technical teams ask first.

Do we need an ML or research team?

No. SynOS platform agents build the training environments and clean the data, which is the work a research team would otherwise do. A small team of domain experts and engineers runs it.

Where do our data and the model live?

In your own cloud account. SynOS installs there and reads your systems in place, and the traces, corrections, training data and model weights are all stored inside your boundary.

Which models does it train?

Open-weight models, chosen per use case. Agents can run on a hosted model while training data accumulates, and every run is priced per model and per provider so you can compare the two.

What do GEPA, SFT and RL with verifiable rewards mean?

They are three ways of improving an agent, applied in this order.

GEPA
Prompt optimisation. It rewrites an agent's instructions using its scored runs and your experts' corrections, before any model weights change.
SFT
Supervised fine-tuning. The model is trained on examples of correct work, including your experts' corrections.
RL with verifiable rewards
The model attempts a task many times and is reinforced on the attempts that pass a check you can verify, such as a matching record or a valid query.
What happens to the model and the data if we stop?

They stay where they are: in your account. The weights, evals, training data and traces are yours whether or not you carry on with SynOS.

How much expert time does it take?

Experts review and correct agent runs in the tools they already use, so the work looks like their normal review of output. How much time that takes depends on the use case and on how often the agent is wrong.

What does a first use case look like?

One workflow your experts already do, with a clear idea of what a correct result is. SynOS connects the systems that workflow touches, agents run on live data with their writes recorded, and the corrections your experts make start the eval set and the training data.

How is this different from a hosted fine-tuning API?

A fine-tuning API starts from a dataset you have already built and trains on the provider's infrastructure. SynOS builds the environment and the dataset from your systems and your experts' corrections, trains inside your cloud and gates each release on your evals.
