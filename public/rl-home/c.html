The rungs most companies stall on.

A training run is the step people picture. Six pieces of work come before it, and a missing one is enough to keep a team on the lower rungs.

  • The training environment

    Agents need somewhere to act on real systems without consequences. Copies and sandboxes drift away from the business they were copied from.

    Handled in The Agent Runtime

  • The data

    Historical records are scattered across systems, inconsistent and unlabelled. What your experts know lives in their heads, outside any dataset.

    Handled in The Brain

  • The reward

    Someone has to decide what "correct" means for each task, and keep that definition current as the work changes.

    Handled in The Training Plane

  • The team

    Research engineers who build environments and run post-training are scarce and costly to hire.

    Handled by platform agents in The Training Plane

  • The loop

    Corrections made in production rarely flow back into the model that made the mistake.

    Handled across The Agent Runtime and The Training Plane

  • Sovereignty

    Training data cannot leave the company's boundary, which rules out sending it to someone else's training service.

    Handled by installing in your own cloud

SynOS is three parts, installed together in your cloud.

Each part feeds the next. The Brain gives agents context, The Agent Runtime turns their work into scored runs and corrections, and The Training Plane turns those into a better model.

  1. Context

    The Brain

    Connects the systems and historical data you already have, resolves the same entity across sources, and holds the procedures your experts follow. Every agent and every training run reads from it.

  2. Runs and corrections

    The Agent Runtime

    Agents run against live data, with their writes intercepted and recorded rather than executed, then scored. Expert corrections are captured in the tools experts already use, and every run is priced per model and per provider.

  3. Your model

    The Training Plane

    Turns runs and corrections into evals and training data, improves agents with prompt optimisation (GEPA), then post-trains an open-weight model with SFT and RL with verifiable rewards. A release that scores worse than the last one does not ship, and the weights are yours.

  • A release that scores worse than the last one does not ship. The weights are yours.

Questions technical teams ask first.

Anything not covered here, bring to the call with your use case.

Do we need an ML or research team?

No. SynOS is built for a small team of domain experts and engineers. Platform agents build the training environments and clean the data, which is the work a research team would otherwise do.

Where do our data and the model live?

In your own cloud account. SynOS installs there and reads your systems in place, and the runs, the training data and the model weights stay inside that boundary.

Which models does it use?

The model you own is an open-weight model that The Training Plane post-trains. While the training data builds up, your agents can also call a hosted model, chosen per workload, and every run is priced per model and per provider.

What are GEPA, SFT and RL with verifiable rewards, in plain words?

GEPA is prompt optimisation: it rewrites an agent's instructions using what went wrong in scored runs, and keeps the versions that score better. SFT, supervised fine-tuning, trains the model on worked examples, including your experts' corrections.

RL with verifiable rewards lets the model attempt a task many times and reinforces the attempts that pass a check you can verify, such as a matching record or a passing eval.

What happens to the model and the data if we stop?

They stay with you. The weights, the evals, the training data and the run history live in your cloud account, and you can keep serving the model you trained.

How much expert time does it take?

Experts review and correct agent work in the tools they already use, as part of the work they already do, so there is no separate labelling project. How much time depends on the use case and how often its work changes, and it is scoped with you before anything starts.

What does a first use case look like?

One workflow your experts already do and can judge, on systems SynOS can read. Agents run it against live data with their writes recorded and scored, your experts correct them, and the first evals and training data come from those corrections.

How is this different from a hosted fine-tuning API?

A hosted fine-tuning API trains on a dataset you have already built, and the model usually runs on the provider's infrastructure. SynOS builds that dataset from your systems and your experts' corrections, gates each release on your evals, and runs in your own cloud, where the weights stay.
