Compare/Search and assistants

What your company wrote, and what your company runs.

Enterprise search tools and memory layers do something real and do it well. The distinction that matters is that the written record is not where the operating truth lives.

Stated fairly

They are better than us at the thing they do.

If your question is where the onboarding policy went or what was decided in that thread, buy one of these. We are not built for it and would lose.

Where they are stronger

  • Ranking across a very large corpus of documents, threads and tickets, with years of tuning behind it
  • Permissions inherited from the source document, which is the right model for content
  • An assistant experience your whole company can use on day one with no configuration
  • Connectors to every place a company writes things down, maintained for you
  • Fast to value, and easy to justify, because everyone recognises the problem

Where it leaves you

  • They index what was written. The state of the business lives in the ledger, the ticket queue and the ERP, and that state changes hourly
  • They assume ingestion. Content is copied in, which is fine for documents and unavailable for the systems that matter most
  • A retrieved passage is not a bound metric. Knowing which column revenue actually means, with invariants on it, is a different piece of work
  • They retrieve and summarise. There is no governed tool layer, no sandbox, no deploy path, and no way for an answer to become an action
  • What accumulates is search behaviour rather than corrections from people doing the work

They work on what your company wrote. This works on what your company runs, and can act inside it.
