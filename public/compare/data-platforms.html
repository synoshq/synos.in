Compare/Data platforms

They are right that this is a context problem. The precondition is where it stalls.

Your warehouse or lakehouse vendor has reached the same conclusion we have: an agent is only as good as the context it is given, and that context has to be governed.

Stated fairly

What a data platform is genuinely better at.

This is not a comparison we would pick if the work sat entirely inside the warehouse.

Where they are stronger

  • Query performance and scale on data that already lives with them, which is not close
  • A mature governance and lineage story for exactly that data
  • Already bought, already reviewed by your security team, already in the budget
  • Deep tooling for analysts, notebooks, dashboards and the whole reporting surface
  • If your critical workflow really is warehouse-shaped, using what you have is the right call

Where it leaves you

  • The systems that hold the operational truth are usually the ones that will never move: the ERP, the ticketing system, the twenty year old ledger, the internal API with no owner
  • A context layer scoped to one platform can only ground answers about the part of the business that migrated
  • Nothing in the warehouse can act. Writing back to a CRM or a campaign is a different problem with a different blast radius
  • The people who hold the operating knowledge are not the people who write SQL, and the gap between them is where the work actually stops
  • A migration is quoted as the path to AI, and by the time it lands the ground has moved

Their answer needs the data inside first. That precondition is the reason you have not solved this already.
