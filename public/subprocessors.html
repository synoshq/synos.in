Subprocessors

Last updated: 5 August 2026

A subprocessor is a third party that processes personal data on our behalf. This page lists every one we use, what it is used for, and — importantly — which deployments it applies to.

Self-hosted deployments: no subprocessors

In a self-hosted deployment — our default, and the mode used for our current enterprise engagements — SynOS runs inside the customer's own infrastructure. Customer data does not reach SynOS Labs LLP, and no subprocessor of ours touches it.

Any model provider, cloud or storage used by such a deployment is selected and contracted by the customer, and is the customer's subprocessor, not ours.

3. Changes to this list

We update this page when the list changes. Unless the signed agreement provides otherwise, customers under contract are notified of any new subprocessor that would process their data at least 30 days before it is engaged, through the contact channel in the agreement, and may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

To be notified of changes, or to request a signed data-processing agreement, email privacy@synos.in.
