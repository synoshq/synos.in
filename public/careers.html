Careers

Hard, unglamorous infrastructure that a lot of companies need and almost nobody is building.

The work here is making messy enterprise data usable without moving it, letting AI act inside systems that were never designed for it, and doing both in a way a security reviewer will sign.

Before anything else

The stage, stated plainly.

People take roles at companies this size on incomplete information, and then discover the parts nobody mentioned. We would rather put them at the top.

Very early, and small A live platform, real engagements, and a team you could fit around one table. Hiring is sequenced against a round that is in progress, so timing on a specific role may not be immediate. We will tell you where things stand on the first call rather than the fourth.
Enterprise pace, startup resources Security reviews, procurement and air-gapped installs are part of the work. That means real deadlines set by other people, and it also means the feedback loop is a customer rather than a metric on a dashboard.
Bengaluru, and in person more often than not The company is based in Bengaluru. This kind of building goes badly when it is fully distributed too early, so we expect meaningful time in the same room.
You will use the product every day We run our own operations, research and go to market on it. That is a good way to work and it also means you will be looking at its rough edges constantly, which some people find energising and some find maddening.

Three areas

We would talk to someone strong in any of these at any time.

These are areas rather than requisitions. If one of them describes you, the conversation is worth having whether or not a role is formally open at that moment.

Infrastructure engineering

founding · Bengaluru

The substrate: profiling and entity resolution across systems with no clean schema, federated query at answer time, the governed tool layer, container isolation, and an evaluation harness that replays an agent against real systems with its writes captured.

What you would work on

  • Making bad data usable in place, which is the deepest problem here
  • The credential, permission and audit surfaces underneath every call
  • Replay and scoring of agent trajectories, including captured writes
  • Air-gapped install, upgrade and revocation paths

Signals we look for

  • You have owned something in production that other people depended on
  • You are careful about failure modes that only appear in month seven
  • You would rather delete a feature than carry a claim you cannot defend
  • Backend depth in Python or a systems language, and comfort near data

Forward-deployed engineering

customer facing · Bengaluru

Landing the layer inside a real company: connecting systems nobody has documented, binding metrics to the columns they actually live in, and getting a first workflow into production alongside the customer's own people.

What you would work on

  • Profiling and binding a customer's systems, then handing that over
  • Teaching subject matter experts to author skills and corrections
  • Turning what you hit repeatedly into product rather than into a runbook
  • Sitting in security reviews and answering honestly

Signals we look for

  • You can hold a technical conversation and a business one in the same hour
  • You have worked inside a customer's environment and enjoyed it
  • You get impatient with doing the same manual thing twice
  • You are comfortable saying we cannot do that yet

Go to market

enterprise · India and US

Selling infrastructure to technical buyers who have been pitched at heavily and have stopped listening.

What you would work on

  • Reaching AI and platform leaders at mid-sized enterprises
  • Turning research conversations into a position that holds up
  • Running the first US motion alongside the India one
  • Partner and channel routes where a services firm builds on this

Signals we look for

  • You have sold something technical that needed a security review
  • You write well, because most of this happens in writing
  • You are comfortable being told no by people you respect
  • You would rather lose a deal than oversell what is built

How to get in touch

Write to us, and say something specific.

Email hello@synos.in with which of the three areas fits and one thing you have built or sold that you would defend in detail.

The process is a conversation with Anoop, then a working session on a real problem from our actual backlog rather than a puzzle, then time with the rest of the team.
