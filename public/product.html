The Brain Layer

Three layers. One institutional brain.

SynOS combines live system context, self-learning from agent executions, and human expertise extraction into a single knowledge layer any agent can draw from.

01

Context Layer — Discovery Agents

Profiling agents automatically analyze your connected data sources. They extract schema, statistics, and business semantics — then discover cross-system relationships that no single system can see. Runs continuously as your data evolves.

02

Self-Learning Layer — Agent Executions

Every time an agent executes, the system captures patterns, exceptions, and edge cases. Those learnings are written back into the brain layer and become available to every other agent. Your 10th agent inherits everything the first 9 learned.

03

Human Context Extraction — Team Review

Your team corrects mistakes in plain language — one sentence at a time. Corrections pass through a quality gate and become permanent institutional memory. Tribal knowledge that lived in people's heads becomes organizational knowledge.

Freshness decay deprioritizes stale information. Conflict detection resolves contradictions. Quality gates prevent noise from entering the knowledge base.

Under the Hood

A typed knowledge graph. Not a flat vector store.

SynOS builds a living knowledge graph from your connected systems — not just embeddings in a vector database. Every node has confidence scores, freshness decay, and source lineage.

Cross-System Entity Resolution

Automatically discovers that "Customer ID" in Salesforce = "Client #" in the spreadsheet = @acmecorp in Slack. Recognizes aliases, maps identities across 50+ tools, and builds a unified entity layer that every agent queries.

Freshness-Aware Knowledge

Not all knowledge is equal over time. Schema insights decay over 90 days. Data quality flags decay over 30 days. A fact from last week scores higher than one from 6 months ago. The system naturally forgets stale information — unlike search engines that index everything forever.

5-Pass Data Profiling

Every connected source goes through 5 automated passes: schema analysis, statistical profiling, semantic extraction, relationship discovery, and knowledge synthesis. No manual data mapping. The brain understands your data shape before any agent queries it.

Hybrid Graph RAG Retrieval

Agents don't get flat document chunks. They get structured context through a 7-stage pipeline: query classification, semantic search, keyword search, seed extraction, graph traversal (up to 3 hops), freshness-weighted RRF fusion, and confidence-based iteration. If the first pass isn't confident enough, the system automatically broadens its search.

Quality-Gated Learning

Not every observation becomes knowledge.

A 3-gate promotion pipeline ensures only high-quality insights enter your institutional brain. This is what separates compounding knowledge from noisy logs.

G1

Corroboration

An insight must be independently observed 2+ times before promotion. A single anomalous observation stays pending — it doesn't contaminate the brain.

G2

Deduplication

If the insight already exists in the knowledge graph, it's merged — not duplicated. Contradictions are flagged for human review rather than silently overwritten.

G3

Classification

Is this generalizable organizational knowledge — or agent-specific noise? Only insights that apply broadly get promoted to the institutional brain. The rest stay as agent-local context.

Three types of knowledge captured

Semantic — facts about your data ("APAC records have 18% null emails"). Episodic — events that matter ("Major spike on March 15 was a compliance audit, not real growth"). Procedural — how-to knowledge ("Steps to verify a customer identity across billing and CRM").

Graduated Trust

Safety and control, from day one to autonomous action.

Every agent starts supervised. You control the dial. Every execution produces human-readable narration — not logs, but a clear explanation of what the agent did and why.

Mode What Happens When to Use
Supervised Plans actions, doesn't execute. See what it would do before anything happens. First days — validate accuracy and judgment
Shadow Reads execute. Writes are logged but don't fire. Build confidence without risk. Week 2-3 — verify write behavior is correct
Autonomous Full execution with per-tool permissions and audit trail. You review exceptions. When accuracy is proven — trust earned, not assumed
